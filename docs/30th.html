💰 MSRP vs. Market Price

MSRP = Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price: the intended retail price. Market price = what sellers are charging on the secondary market. Because many 30th products are unreleased, today's market numbers are presale prices and can be heavily inflated.

Use the Target Buy column as your main protection against overpaying. Promo values are not guaranteed and can fall after release.

⚠️ How to think about “getting your money back”

A promo does not guarantee that you'll recover the product price. Especially before release, promo and chase-card values can move dramatically once large quantities enter the market.

The safest strategy is to buy close to MSRP, then let the included promo/extras reduce your effective cost. For opening, prioritize products with a strong pack count and low cost per pack rather than paying a huge secondary-market premium.
